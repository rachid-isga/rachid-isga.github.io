Project Overview

FLDM Master Application is an enterprise higher-education platform built to manage candidate applications, transcript evaluations, academic tracks, semester timetables, and student grading appeals. Built to withstand extreme traffic during admission seasons, it leverages Yajra server-side DataTables, Fast-Excel for dossier exports, and Owen-It Auditing for strict compliance.

Problem & Solution

The Challenge

Evaluating thousands of applicant dossiers simultaneously creates bottlenecks for university jury committees, while paper admission slips are prone to forgery and grading disputes need transparent auditing.

The Engineered Solution

The system delivers high-speed server-side candidate grids, automated QR-coded PDF convocation slips via mPDF, room/building scheduling modules, and comprehensive audit logs recording every administrative dossier state change.
