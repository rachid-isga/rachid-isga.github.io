Project Overview

OlympusStaff is a high-concurrency content publishing and reading platform built for digital comics, manhwa, and manga enthusiasts. Operating in live production, it delivers an optimized continuous vertical reader, user bookmarking libraries, VIP tier chapter gating with proof verification, configurable ad placement zones, and an anti-scraping IP shield.

Problem & Solution

The Challenge

Manga websites experience heavy bandwidth load from thousands of high-resolution chapter images, aggressive content scrapers stealing images, and the challenge of sustainably monetizing releases through subscription tiers.

The Engineered Solution

OlympusStaff integrates Intervention Image v3 for automated WebP compression and chunked preloading, dynamic IP blacklisting and crawler detection (`BlockedIp`), and a VIP subscription verification workflow with configurable ad spaces (`AdPlacement`).
