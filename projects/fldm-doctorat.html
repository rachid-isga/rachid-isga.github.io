Academic Management System

FLDM Gestion Doctorat

Doctoral research management platform for USMBA Faculty of Letters & Human Sciences Dhar El Mahraz, managing candidate admissions, CED laboratory structures, thesis topics, and Arabic attestations.

Primary Stack PHP 8.2, Laravel 12, ar-php, DomPDF, Maatwebsite Excel, MySQL
Special Features Bilingual Arabic/French Typography & Excel Batch Importers
Target Users CED Directorate, Research Laboratories, Doctoral Students & Supervisors

Project Overview

FLDM Gestion Doctorat is an academic information system built for managing doctoral programs at the Faculty of Letters and Human Sciences Dhar El Mahraz (Sidi Mohamed Ben Abdellah University). It unifies candidate registration, supervisor (encadrant) assignment, doctoral study center (CED) formations, thesis topic (sujet) validation, and automated generation of official Moroccan academic certificates with bilingual Arabic/French support.

Problem & Solution

The Challenge

Universities struggle with managing multi-year doctoral research milestones, tracking supervisor quotas, and generating official government attestations that require accurate bidirectional Arabic names and titles without character corruption.

The Engineered Solution

The system maps the complete university research hierarchy (CED -> Structures -> Formations -> Sujets -> Etudiants), provides Maatwebsite Excel batch ingestion, and embeds Khaled.Alshamaa/ar-php with DomPDF for generating official Moroccan academic documents.
