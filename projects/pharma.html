Pharma ERP

Comprehensive pharmacy enterprise management system featuring Point of Sale (POS), physical cash register balancing, batch expiry date tracking, third-party insurer billing, and inter-pharmacy confrere item loans.

Primary Stack PHP 8.2, Laravel 12, Yajra DataTables, DomPDF, mPDF, Maatwebsite Excel, MySQL
Database Complexity 92 Migrations & Over 70 Normalized Relational Models
Domain Scope Pharmacy POS, Inventory FEFO, Caisse Ledger & Insurer Claims

Project Overview

Pharma is an end-to-end Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platform developed for pharmacies and medical dispensaries. Built with over 90 database migrations and 70+ Eloquent models, it manages everything from high-speed barcode checkout and physical cash drawers (Caisses) to supplier purchase orders, delivery notes (BL), batch expiration tracking, insurer co-pay processing, and peer pharmacy loans.

Problem & Solution

The Challenge

Pharmacies lose substantial revenue from untracked expiring medication batches, cash register discrepancies, unregularized peer pharmacy loans, and tedious manual health insurance co-pay calculations.

The Engineered Solution

Pharma introduces automated FEFO expiration warnings (`Datepremptionproduit`), real-time cash drawer session reconciliation, confrere debt/credit tracking, active pharmaceutical ingredient (DCI) counter-indication alerts, and instant PDF invoice dispatch.
