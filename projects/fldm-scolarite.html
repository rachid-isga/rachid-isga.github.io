Project Overview

FLDM ScolaritÃ© Gestion is a dedicated administrative application engineered for university schooling and registrar services. It manages undergraduate student identities, CNE/MASSAR national student records, academic tracks (Parcours), exam seating arrangements, and automated student password resets.

Problem & Solution

The Challenge

Organizing exams for tens of thousands of university students requires assigning unique exam seating roll numbers across exam halls without collision, resetting student passwords before exam sessions, and migrating inconsistent historical SQL datasets.

The Engineered Solution

The system pairs a registrar management interface with high-performance Laravel Artisan CLI automation scripts to process bulk records in memory-efficient chunks, generate exam numbers deterministically, and migrate legacy university schemas safely.
