Deutschio Platform

German language learning LMS featuring structured video courses, sequential quizzes, automated DomPDF certificates, teacher consultations, and digital book library.

Primary Stack PHP 8.2, Laravel 12, Spatie Permissions, DomPDF, Tailwind CSS, Alpine.js
System Type Educational LMS & Consultation Engine
Database Entities 28 Migrations (Courses, Quizzes, Certificates, Consultations)

Project Overview

Deutschio Platform is an all-in-one educational web platform built for German language students (levels A1 to C2). It combines video curricula structured into Chapters and Lessons with automated grammar assessments, digital certificate rendering, and a teacher booking engine that allows students to reserve 1-on-1 consultations based on teacher availability calendars.

Problem & Solution

The Challenge

Language learners often navigate fragmented tools: watching videos on one site, taking quizzes elsewhere, and booking tutors over messaging apps, leading to lost progress and poor retention tracking.

The Engineered Solution

Deutschio unifies student enrollment, lesson completion tracking, sequential quiz checkpoints, instant PDF certificate generation upon passing, and a consultation booking engine with teacher availability calendars.
