FLDM Institutional Website

Official faculty web portal featuring dynamic CMS, fast student exam results lookup, bilingual French/Arabic publishing, and emergency maintenance controls.

Primary Stack PHP 8.2, Laravel 12, MySQL, Tailwind CSS, Blade, Vite
Database Entities 33 Migrations (News, Events, Programs, Results, Staff, Audits)
Special Controls EmergencyShutdownController for High-Traffic Exam Peaks

Project Overview

The FLDM Website serves as the central digital portal for the Faculty of Letters and Human Sciences Dhar El Mahraz. It provides dynamic content management for news, academic departments, and announcements, while delivering a high-speed exam results search engine and emergency load protection controllers.
